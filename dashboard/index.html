Reading the radar

The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record, plus 157 more pre-token protocols below that floor. The board ranks only genuine launch candidates, under the same exclusion the watchlist uses: no wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, and no exchange product — a bridge for BTC or a staked-ETH receipt has no token to launch.

Implied value is a model's estimate of what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It is not a price, not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued, and not investment advice. The growth term is asymmetric on purpose: a shrinking protocol loses value faster than a growing one gains it, because a launch cannot rescue a shrinking user base. Young protocols are discounted for launch uncertainty; multi-chain protocols earn a small stickiness bonus.
